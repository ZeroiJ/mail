# Feature Logic & User Workflows

## High-Speed Triage
*   **Triage-First Queue:** Instead of an endless infinite scroll, implement a finite daily inbox or a card-deck swipe view to hit "Inbox Zero" in under two minutes[cite: 1]. 
*   **Gesture Mapping:** Swipe left to delete, right to archive, up to snooze[cite: 1]. 

## Reading Experience & Privacy
*   **Reader Mode for Newsletters:** Automatically strip heavy HTML tables, inline styles, and marketing headers[cite: 1]. Convert rich marketing emails into clean Markdown-style plain text with legible typography[cite: 1].
*   **Tracker & Spy Pixel Stripping:** Detect and neutralize $1\times 1$ transparent tracking pixels before loading images, preventing senders from knowing when or where you opened an email[cite: 1].

## Automation & Focus
*   **Ephemeral / Self-Destructing OTPs:** Auto-detect 4–8 digit verification codes and order updates, displaying them in a glanceable card and auto-deleting the email after 24 hours[cite: 1].
*   **VIP-Only Push Notifications:** Configure notifications to trigger only if an email arrives from a starred contact or contains specific urgent keywords (e.g., "urgent", "offer", "contract")[cite: 1].
*   **On-Device Thread TL;DR:** Use local on-device processing (such as Gemini Nano on supported devices) to generate a 2-sentence bullet summary of long conversation threads[cite: 1].
*   **Extracted "Action Cards":** Parse flight numbers, meeting invitations, package tracking IDs, and calendar dates into interactive widgets pinned to the top of the message thread[cite: 1].
